# DynamicFeeHook

- Base and scope: `PoolBoundTruncatedOracleV2`, with the unchanged `PoolBoundHookParametersV1` constructor. The optional oracle and internal arithmetic compile into this one hook. Author-payment, Dynamic model, initial capacity and read-only rate declarations are overridden; callbacks, custody, payees and accounting remain inherited. The core remains oracle-free and deploys no validation helper. Pool LP fees must be zero.
- Signal: retain two genuine observations automatically. Let `latest` and `previous` be the newest adjacent ring samples. Their modular signed cumulative difference divided by their modular uint32 timestamp interval recovers the previously held truncated tick exactly. `D = currentTruncatedTick - previousHeldTick`; `E = uint32(now) - previous.timestamp`, including idle age. `K` is the frozen configured per-block tick clamp. Fewer than two samples or a zero-length interval means no velocity signal. Quote normalization makes a positive `D` an upward quote-per-base price movement.
- Formula: `M = maximumPips`, `B = floor(M / 5)`, `C = K * E`. For `D > 0`, `E > 0` and `K > 0`, return `B + floor((M - B) * min(D * 30, C) / C)`. A full allowed tick move within 30 seconds reaches `M`; smaller or slower rises produce less uplift. The view returns zero before oracle reads when `M == 0`. Missing history, flat/falling movement or unavailable elapsed time/capacity returns `B`. Exact input/output, direction, request size, reserves and fee currency mode do not change this schedule.
- Arithmetic and freezing: saturation checks `D >= ceil(C / 30)` before multiplying an arbitrary signed signal. `C < 2^56`; below saturation the uplift numerator is below `2^80`, and the result never exceeds uint24 `M`. Every division floors nonnegative rate arithmetic. The base rejects an `int256.min` request and enforces the configured maximum. It freezes the rate before oracle observers and uses that scalar throughout settlement, so a swap's own price impact does not change its charge. Preview remains a current-state estimate, not a guaranteed future quote.
- Author payment: `authorFeeBps()` requires 500 bps of source-attributed owner proceeds after bounty through the existing V3 hub, independent of trading fees. No payee is added. A different frozen author allocation is rejected by inherited validation.
- Provenance: independently authored from the disclosed upward-velocity schedule; no external fee-hook implementation was copied. MIT; dependency licenses remain applicable.
- Risks: this measures the last observed truncated movement, not raw spot velocity, statistical volatility or a long-window TWAP. Clamping and pre-swap sampling introduce lag; a truncated feed may keep catching up while raw spot is flat. Warm-up deliberately charges baseline rather than inventing history. Idle time gradually fades a previous rise toward baseline. Clamping does not guarantee manipulation resistance, and arbitrary inherited author code is not a sandbox. Only 3 runtime bytes remain below EIP-170 under the pinned compiler; policy changes require a new size check, artifact review and mined salt.
- Qualification: 12 deterministic/fuzz arithmetic tests pass. Real-fork launches exercise both fee modes and exact-input/output directions, frozen-rate accounting, genuine history and royalty payouts. The targeted example scenario observed rates of 10,000 pips during an upward truncated movement, 3,818 after idle time, and 2,000 after falling/flat observations. Core-only and composed fixtures also pass registration ABI-envelope regressions. This reference has null author identity and is not production admission or author-control proof.
